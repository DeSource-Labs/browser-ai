# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Give technical feedback about the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when needed, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, trolling, and sustained disruption are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to [hello@desource-labs.org](mailto:hello@desource-labs.org). Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect the reporter's privacy where possible, and take proportionate action. Do not use public issues for sensitive conduct reports.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participation when behavior harms the community.

This policy is based on the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
